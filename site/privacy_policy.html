Privacy Policy for BattMan

Effective date: October 31, 2026

BattMan is operated by Cyril Courtiat (“the developer”). This policy explains how BattMan handles information when you use the app.

Information processed by the app

BattMan reads battery and device information, such as battery level, charging status, temperature, and screen-related settings, to display its battery report and related features. This information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

BattMan does not require an account. The app does not collect, store, or share personal information through its normal operation. It does not collect precise location information.

Information you send to the developer

If you contact the developer by email, the developer will receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and message contents. That information will be used to respond to you and will not be used for advertising. You may ask the developer to delete your message by contacting cyril.ingenierie@gmail.com. The developer may retain information as needed to respond or meet legal obligations.

Third-party services

BattMan does not send your battery or device readings to third parties. If this changes, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant services and data practices.

Children’s privacy

BattMan is not designed for children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its normal operation. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer by email, contact cyril.ingenierie@gmail.com to request its deletion.

Security

BattMan processes its battery and device readings on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. The developer takes reasonable steps to protect information received directly by email.

Changes to this policy

The developer may update this policy. The latest version will be posted at the policy URL, along with its effective date. If a change affects how BattMan handles information, the app and policy description will be updated as appropriate.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Cyril Courtiat at cyril.ingenierie@gmail.com.
